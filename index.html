Our mission

To nurture values-based excellence and a passion for helping catalyse improvements in society.

We focus on solutions that are innovative, yet pragmatic and effective. Most of our work happens through partners: NGOs who know their communities, and whom we support with grants, guidance and connections.

How we choose partners

Five things we look for.

Every programme we support shares these characteristics. They are also how we hold ourselves to account.

  1. 01

    Values

    Integrity, and respect for the communities a programme serves, come first.

  2. 02

    Effectiveness

    Programmes that deliver results on the ground, not only on paper.

  3. 03

    Validation

    Evidence that an approach works, and the discipline to keep measuring it.

  4. 04

    Innovation

    New ideas that are pragmatic enough to work, and to grow.

  5. 05

    Excellence

    The pursuit of quality in every activity, however humble.

What partnership looks like.

Healthcare · Assam

A floating clinic on the Brahmaputra

A boat clinic brings dental, eye and ultrasound care to tribal and riverine villages in Biswanath and Lakhimpur, with a goal of cutting infant mortality by at least 10% in a year.

Karuna Trust
Healthcare · Wardha

A heritage library, rebuilt for the next generation of rural doctors

The Dr APJ Kalam Library at MGIMS Sewagram grew from 2,700 to 16,000 square feet, keeping the heritage structure intact.

Dr APJ Kalam Library, MGIMS
Nurturing Excellence · Karnataka

India’s fastest sprinters, from the back of beyond

Bridges of Sports finds and trains young sprinters from Siddi and other tribal communities in Uttara Kannada, with its sights on the 2028 Olympics.
