What we fund

Four areas where patient funding changes lives.

We work through 51 partner organisations in 8 states and across India. Each area has its own focus, and the same five tests apply to all of them.

01 · 13 partners

Healthcare

Care that reaches the last mile.

Primary care, mental health, eye care, nutrition and medical education for communities the health system struggles to reach. Our partners run a floating clinic on the Brahmaputra, district mental health programmes in Nagpur and community health work in Gadchiroli.

  • Dr APJ Kalam Library, MGIMS
  • Healing Fields Foundation
  • Institute for Psychological Health
  • Karuna Trust
  • Prayas
  • Sankara Nethralaya
  • +7 more
Explore healthcare
02 · 23 partners

Education & Training

Learning that changes what is possible.

Early childhood care, foundational literacy, girls’ education, school leadership and youth training. From Anganwadis in Yavatmal to classrooms in Rajasthan, we back partners who prove what works and then help it grow.

  • 321 Education Foundation
  • Akanksha
  • Akshar
  • Anand Niketan
  • ARCH Dharampur
  • Bridges of Sports
  • +17 more
Explore education
03 · 8 partners

Livelihood Enhancement

Income, water and dignity for rural families.

Watershed development, women’s collectives, sustainable farming and enterprise. Our partners help rural and tribal households build incomes that last, and the institutions to protect them.

  • Amhi Amchya Arogyasathi
  • BAIF Institute of Sustainable Livelihoods and Development
  • Chetana Vikas
  • Family Welfare Agency
  • Gramin Vikas Vigyan Samiti
  • PRADAN
  • +2 more
Explore livelihoods
04 · 8 partners

Nurturing Excellence

Backing exceptional people and ideas.

Sprinters from the back of beyond, women leaders in Gram Panchayats, khadi artisans, tiny forests on degraded land and a 24-book series on Indian lives. This is where we back excellence in its many forms.

  • Bridges of Sports
  • Farmers For Forests
  • Gram Sewa Mandal
  • HaqDarshak
  • HarperCollins India
  • Movement for Scavenger Community
  • +2 more
Explore excellence

Doing work that deserves to grow?

We partner with organisations building innovative, pragmatic and cost-effective solutions for India’s rural and tribal communities. We also welcome philanthropists, companies and foundations who want to co-fund what works.
