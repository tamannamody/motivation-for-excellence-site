Privacy

Privacy notice

Plain language, kept short.

What we collect

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If you write to us, we receive what you choose to send: usually your name, email address, organisation and message.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and to consider any partnership you propose. We do not sell or share them for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as it is needed for the purpose you wrote to us about, and then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by writing toinfo@motivationforexcellence.org.
